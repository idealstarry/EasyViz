The associated single-page PDFs were independently inspected for page dimensions and extracted text typography. There is no accepted baseline or supplied publication reference for this probe. The inputs are 165 synthetic rows prepared after the initial runtime freeze. This inspection establishes visible route differences and local readability only. It does not establish model effectiveness, causal improvement, untested generalization or journal acceptance. No aesthetic winner is declared.

## Numerical and export evidence

- **Passed independently:** 165 input rows across the five cases; all 14 PDFs have one page, a measured page width of 80.000000 mm and height of approximately 70.000002 mm, and extracted text spans use `ArialMT` at 8.0 pt. The small height difference is floating-point representation.
- **Passed from supplied source-to-artist records, not independently recomputed:** all raw observations/coordinates and marker areas retained; means and sample SD unchanged; matrix cell values and global 0–50 limits unchanged. Image inspection found no conflicting visible label or scale mapping.
- **Failed:** the supplied and visible initial raw-point separation of the quartile and density cases.
- **Not checked:** statistical design suitability beyond the adopted captions, font-subset embedding, physical printing, color-vision accessibility, model generalization, publication-level aesthetics and any causal improvement over a no-skill baseline.

No clipping, contradictory legend key, contour/summary crossing, unreadable label or other required visual repair was found in the means, matrix or coordinate cases at the reviewed dimensions. The small matrix intentionally preserves legible square cells; this does not establish a preferred layout for other matrix sizes. All five route comparisons have `no_clear_preference`: no winner is required to establish their bounded functional differences.

Overall status: **`needs_revision`**, restricted to the raw-observation packing in the quartile and density cases. Keep these initial images and their failed QA records as evidence, and evaluate revised files separately.
