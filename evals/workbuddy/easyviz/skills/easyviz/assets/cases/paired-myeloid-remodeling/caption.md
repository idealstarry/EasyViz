Paired changes in source-reported myeloid deconvolution scores after bariatric surgery. All 16 myeloid subtypes are shown for the Petrus cohort (15 participants) and Kerr cohort (37 participants). Changes are calculated within each participant as the two-year score minus the baseline score. Source scores and subgroup annotations are adapted from Massier et al., Nature Communications 14, 1438 (2023), doi:10.1038/s41467-023-36983-2, under CC BY 4.0. No deconvolution was rerun. Scores are not cell percentages, and the two cohorts are summarized separately.

For the conventional candidate, boxes indicate the first and third quartiles, black segments indicate medians, whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and points show all paired changes. In the participant-matrix candidate, each column represents one participant, kept in the same position across all rows. Participants are sorted within each cohort by their median change across the 16 displayed subtypes; this ordering is descriptive, not an independently discovered cluster. The continuous color scale is symmetric around zero and includes every observed value without clipping or row standardization. The aligned margin shows cohort medians and interquartile ranges. In the distribution-ledger candidate, points show all participant changes, larger points show medians, thick segments show interquartile ranges, and the right columns show the percentage of paired changes strictly below zero (P, Petrus; K, Kerr). Interquartile ranges describe variation among participants and are not confidence intervals. No significance tests are shown.

Source subtype labels are abbreviated for display: myC, myeloid cluster; LAM, lipid-associated macrophage; Mo, monocyte; DC2, dendritic cell type 2. Vertical spacing between row groups follows the published subtype annotations; it does not indicate newly estimated distances.

For the separate five-year transfer panel, only the 37 Kerr participants are included, and the change is five years minus baseline. The same row definitions, units, color scale and summary definitions apply; columns are re-ordered using the five-year changes.
