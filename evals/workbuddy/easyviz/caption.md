# Cell-type detected fraction and prepared score by treatment arm

Dot area encodes the detected fraction for each cell type and treatment arm, and dot color encodes the prepared score. Dot area is proportional to the detected fraction on a fixed 0–1 scale: the largest plotted area is 165 pt² and the area of each dot is `detected fraction / 1 × 165 pt²`; the encoding is area, not radius, so a dot twice the fraction has twice the area. Both measured zero fractions (resident macrophages under control and cycling myeloid cells under low dose) are retained and drawn with zero area, marked by a separate grey horizontal tick; the tick is a non-quantitative presence flag and does not represent a small positive value. The smallest positive fraction (0.05) is plotted with its exact proportional area. Color follows the prepared score on a single sequential blue scale spanning the observed range, −0.22 to 1.66, with no diverging center; higher scores are darker. The plotting table retains all 24 cell type × treatment observations in the order in which the categories first appear in the source.

The table is synthetic engineering test data supplied with prepared descriptive quantities. The denominator used to compute the detected fraction is not provided, and the construction of the prepared score is not defined; the scale and any interpretation of these values therefore remain unknown. No independent experimental replicates are available, so no statistical test, uncertainty estimate, or inference is presented, and no values were imputed, aggregated, or filtered.

Panel dimensions are 120 × 90 mm with Arial 8 pt text and 300 dpi raster output. Color mapping uses the EasyViz `notch2-blue` sequential scale (light blue to #2581B9), chosen for a visible light endpoint so low-score borderless dots remain distinguishable from the background. The size legend keys use the same value-to-area mapping as the plotted dots.
