Changes in normalized log2 protein abundance from baseline at weeks 4 and 12 in the Control (blue) and Treatment (amber) arms. Each faint circle represents one participant's follow-up value minus their own baseline value for the same protein; vertical jitter separates overlapping observations and has no quantitative meaning. Diamonds mark medians, and horizontal segments span the 25th to 75th percentiles (linear interpolation), describing variation between participants rather than confidence intervals. Zero denotes no change; negative and positive values indicate lower and higher abundance relative to baseline, respectively. The same horizontal scale is used for both visits. Protein order follows the source table. Baseline measurements were available for 12 participants per arm; for each of the six proteins, 11 participants per arm contributed paired changes at week 4 and 10 per arm at week 12. Each time point uses its available baseline–follow-up pairs, so the contributing participants may differ between visits. Six unmeasured follow-up visits across the two arms, corresponding to 36 absent protein measurements, were not imputed. All 252 observed baseline–follow-up protein pairs are shown. No upstream reanalysis, inferential tests, or multiple-comparison procedures were performed. Source: user-supplied `source.csv`. Protein identifiers are retained as supplied: IL6, interleukin 6; CXCL10, C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 10; TNF, tumor necrosis factor; CCL2, C-C motif chemokine ligand 2; IL1RN, interleukin 1 receptor antagonist; VEGFA, vascular endothelial growth factor A.
