Reference: Truong et al., Nature Methods 21, 455–464 (2024), DOI 10.1038/s41592-023-02162-w, Figure 1b.

The three colored components are cell percentages with green signal only (mutEJ), both signals (HDR and mutEJ), or red signal only (HDR). Gray bars use a separate axis for the supplied HDR/mutEJ ratio. Bars summarize the mean and standard deviation across 3 biological replicates. The two controls have too few fluorescence events for their ratios to be informative; those ratio bars are shaded. Asterisks and comparison lines report selected post-hoc comparisons after ANOVA, using Bonferroni adjustment. No experimental procedure is supplied for this graphical reading.
