Figure 1d of Shi et al., Nature Communications (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-22092-5.

Caption evidence, paraphrased: stationary-phase E. coli MG1655 cells were diluted onto an agarose pad containing fresh LB and followed by single-cell time-lapse microscopy. The displayed solid lines and shaded regions represent means and standard deviations from 146 single cells. The width and length trajectories resemble the dynamics measured in the bulk culture.
