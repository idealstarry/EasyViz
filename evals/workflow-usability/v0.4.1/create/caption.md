Mouse-level fluorescence distributions in the current synthetic teaching
experiment. Each dot represents one independently sampled mouse (12 mice per
arm; 36 mice total), using the arithmetic mean of its available measured
technical reads. One instrument-failed read was missing; its mouse (0008) was
retained using its single measured read. Signal was already background-corrected
and is reported in arbitrary units (a.u.). Boxes span the 25th to 75th
percentiles, with the median shown as a line; whiskers extend to the most extreme
observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges. All mouse-level observations,
including values beyond the whiskers, are drawn. Categorical displacement
separates nearby dots without changing signals.

Two prespecified, two-sided Mann–Whitney comparisons assessed each dose arm
against Vehicle, with Holm adjustment across this single family of two tests.
Signals were higher in both dose arms: Low dose, Cliff's δ = 0.708 and adjusted
P = 0.00355; High dose, δ = 0.972 and adjusted P = 0.000120. Positive δ means
P(dose > Vehicle) − P(dose < Vehicle). P values use the tie-corrected normal
approximation with continuity correction. These are distribution comparisons,
not general tests of equal medians. No effect confidence interval was computed;
box ranges describe observed spread rather than inferential uncertainty. Source
roles and independent units follow the supplied DATA_DICTIONARY.md. The separate
historical pilot and instrument QC readings were not pooled or subtracted.
