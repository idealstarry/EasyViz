## Figure caption

Horizontal interval plot of the **Prepared ratio** for seven synthetic engineering readouts, stratified by preparation batch. Each point marks the supplied Prepared ratio and each line spans the pre-supplied asymmetric 95% interval exactly as provided; intervals were not recomputed, and no fitting, weighting, or statistical testing was performed. The x axis is log-scaled with a dashed reference line at 1. Colors decode the preparation batch (Batch A, blue; Batch B, orange). Points are drawn hollow when the supplied interval includes 1 and filled when it excludes 1; this fill state reports only reference overlap and carries no claim of statistical significance. Independent sample sizes and the method used to construct the intervals were not provided. Three readout–batch combinations were not supplied in the source table (Background suppression × Batch B, Temperature sensitivity × Batch A, Storage response × Batch B) and are left as empty row positions; their absence does not imply that measurement was attempted. Data are synthetic.
