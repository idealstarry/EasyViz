BMI distributions in the Kerr, Krieg, and Petrus cohorts. Filled boxes show Q1–Q3 and the median; whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 IQR of the quartiles. Quartiles use the linear interpolation convention. All 129 supplied participants are shown as smaller neutral dots in category-associated side lanes (Kerr 77, Krieg 23, Petrus 29). The filled summaries lead the center/spread comparison. BMI is in kg/m² and the adopted axis range is 15–60. Points move only along the categorical axis for packing; every literal person_key and measured BMI is retained. No KDE, fitted model or test is adopted.
