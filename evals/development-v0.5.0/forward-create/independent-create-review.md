## Evidence actually inspected

I opened all ten final PNGs as complete canvases, read their separate captions, frozen requests and adopted settings/specifications, and read the latest review packets' measured-check records. I then opened direct 96 dpi PDF renders at the agreed physical proportions. All ten PNG/SVG/PDF hash triplets match the manifest. All eight frozen input CSV hashes match their declared input hashes.

Direct actual SVG inspection confirms the adopted physical dimensions and Arial 8 pt primary text. Direct `pdffonts` inspection confirms embedded ArialMT in every final PDF. Task 06's mathematical tick exponents are 5.6 pt within the 8 pt tick labels; this is a superscript treatment, not a reduced axis/tick role or an omitted font check. No in-image narrative title, overview count or explanatory footnote was found. Captions carry definitions and consequential caveats separately.

The 96 dpi renders are screen proxies for physical panel size, not printed-paper proofs. Primary labels remained readable in those proxies. No supplied accepted baseline or paper/reference image is bound to these Create packets. Comparative preference against an accepted baseline is therefore **not_checked**. The task-specific design benefits below are visible reading mechanisms, not evidence of aesthetic superiority over an unseen baseline.
