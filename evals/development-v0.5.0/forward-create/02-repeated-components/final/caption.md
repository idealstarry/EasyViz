Absolute magnitudes of the three supplied repair outcomes within each treatment. Separate open bars show arithmetic means for HDR, HDR + mutEJ, and mutEJ; each bar retains its three biological-repeat values and sample SD (ddof = 1), giving 45 measurements across five treatments. All series share the adopted 0–55% axis and repeat the same order within each treatment. HDR denotes homology-directed repair and mutEJ the supplied mutagenic end-joining outcome. The joint outcome is the supplied joint_pct, and no denominator is inferred. Outcomes are separate quantities and are not stacked or normalized. No test or significance annotation is adopted.
