Cell width and cell length during single-cell time-lapse imaging of
stationary-phase Escherichia coli MG1655 cells diluted onto an agarose pad
containing fresh LB. Blue and orange lines show the supplied mean width
and length, respectively; shaded regions show the supplied mean ± standard
deviation from 146 followed cells. Every summary at minutes 1–60 is displayed.
Width and length use separately labelled y axes and different numerical
ranges; equal vertical heights do not imply equal dimensions. No fitted
model, test, interpolation of new measurements or SD-to-SEM conversion is
performed. Source summaries and the reference are adapted from Shi et al.,
Nature Communications (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-22092-5, under CC BY 4.0.
The reported cells are observational units within the described experiment,
not an assumed count of independent biological replicates.
