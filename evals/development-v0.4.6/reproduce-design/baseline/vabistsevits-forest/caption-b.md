Direct effects of mammographic-density phenotypes on breast-cancer outcomes accounting for childhood body size. Circles show the 24 published odds ratios per SD increment in the indicated exposure; horizontal segments show the supplied 95% confidence intervals from multivariable inverse-variance-weighted Mendelian randomization. The horizontal axis is logarithmic, and the dashed reference is an odds ratio of 1. Hollow and filled circles retain the source table's interval-overlap states. The supplied estimates and asymmetric endpoints are unchanged. Per-estimate sample sizes are not supplied and remain unknown; caption-level GWAS counts must not be assigned to individual points. ER, estrogen receptor; HER2, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2; SD, standard deviation; CI, confidence interval. Reconstructed from Figure 3b and its Source Data in Vabistsevits et al., Nature Communications 15, 4021 (2024), [DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-48105-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48105-7), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
