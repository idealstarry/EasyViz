Truong et al., Nature Methods (2024), Fig. 1b Source Data (DOI 10.1038/s41592-023-02162-w; CC BY 4.0). Individual panels compare treatments within each author-supplied outcome: HDR (left) and mutEJ (right). Neutral open bars show arithmetic means; intervals show sample SD (ddof = 1), and dots retain all three biological replicates ($n = 3$) per treatment and outcome. The panels use different, explicitly labeled original-percent ranges (0–15% and 0–55%), so bar heights must not be compared across panels as equal absolute amounts. They are individual 72 × 48 mm manuscript panels; the side-by-side preview preserves their sizes and 8 pt type. DMSO is the vehicle treatment; NU = NU-7441 (1 µM), KU = KU-0060548 (0.25 µM), L755 = L755507 (5 µM), with SCR7 omitted in this bounded transfer. Full source condition names, concentrations, value strings and workbook-cell identities remain in the plotting tables. The ‘No donor/Cas9’ and ‘NTC’ controls are excluded from this declared four-treatment subset. No normalization, cross-condition pairing, hypothesis test or significance mark is introduced.
