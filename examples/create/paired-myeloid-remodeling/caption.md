Paired changes in source-reported myeloid deconvolution scores after bariatric surgery. All 16 myeloid subtypes are shown for the Petrus cohort (15 participants) and Kerr cohort (37 participants). Changes are calculated within each participant as the two-year score minus the baseline score. Source scores and subgroup annotations are adapted from Massier et al., Nature Communications 14, 1438 (2023), doi:10.1038/s41467-023-36983-2, under CC BY 4.0. No deconvolution was rerun. Scores are not cell percentages, and the two cohorts are summarized separately.

For the conventional candidate, outlined boxes indicate the first and third quartiles, black segments indicate medians, whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and opaque points show all paired changes. In the participant-matrix candidate, each column represents one participant, kept in the same position across all rows. Participants are sorted within each cohort by their median change across the 16 displayed subtypes; this ordering is descriptive, not an independently discovered cluster. The continuous color scale is symmetric around zero and includes every observed value without clipping or row standardization. The aligned margin shows small outlined first-to-third-quartile boxes with dark median ticks. In the distribution-ledger candidate, two aligned cohort facets use identical change limits and ticks. Opaque points show every participant change at its exact quantitative coordinate; deterministic vertical packing separates nearby values within each row. Small outlined boxes in a separate strip below the points indicate the first and third quartiles, with dark ticks marking medians. The percentage column beside each cohort reports paired changes strictly below zero, with all 15 or 37 matched participants as the denominator. Interquartile ranges describe variation among participants and are not confidence intervals. No significance tests are shown.

Blue denotes Petrus and coral denotes Kerr. This mapping adapts the blue/coral vector stroke colors observed in Figure 2 of the scWAT study (doi:10.1038/s41467-023-43021-8); the compact marks and axes are also informed by Figure 2 of Vanneste et al., Nature Immunology (2023), doi:10.1038/s41590-023-01468-3. These are visual design adaptations rather than shared biological groups or analysis methods. Hollow participant circles were evaluated in the same geometry; the denser rows could not accommodate distinct hollow outlines, so small fully opaque filled circles are used consistently.

Source subtype labels are abbreviated for display: myC, myeloid cluster; LAM, lipid-associated macrophage; Mo, monocyte; DC2, dendritic cell type 2. Vertical spacing between row groups follows the published subtype annotations; it does not indicate newly estimated distances.

For the separate five-year transfer panel, only the 37 Kerr participants are included, and the change is five years minus baseline. The same row definitions, units, color scale and summary definitions apply; columns are re-ordered using the five-year changes.
