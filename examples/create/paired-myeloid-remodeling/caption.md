Paired changes in source-reported myeloid deconvolution scores after bariatric surgery. All 16 myeloid subtypes are shown for the Petrus cohort (15 participants) and Kerr cohort (37 participants), giving 832 matched participant–subtype changes. Changes are calculated within each participant as the two-year score minus the baseline score. Source scores and subtype annotations are adapted from Massier et al., Nature Communications 14, 1438 (2023), doi:10.1038/s41467-023-36983-2, under CC BY 4.0. No deconvolution was rerun. Scores are not cell percentages, and the cohorts are summarized separately.

For the conventional candidate, outlined boxes indicate the first and third quartiles, black segments indicate medians, whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and opaque points show all paired changes. In the participant-matrix candidate, each column represents one participant, kept in the same position across all rows. Participants are sorted within each cohort by their median change across the 16 displayed subtypes; this ordering is descriptive, not an independently discovered cluster. The continuous color scale is symmetric around zero and includes every observation without clipping or row standardization. The aligned margin shows outlined first-to-third-quartile boxes with dark median ticks.

In the distribution-ledger candidate, every change uses one common quantitative axis. Upper blue participant tracks represent Petrus and lower coral tracks represent Kerr. Opaque circles show all exact values; only vertical positions are packed to separate nearby circles. Unfilled first-to-third-quartile boxes and black median ticks occupy separate lanes between the participant tracks. Row heights are measured from the raw circle extents, without changing subtype order. Vertical spacing has no quantitative meaning. The two aligned percentage columns report paired changes strictly below zero, with all 15 or 37 participants as the respective denominators. Interquartile ranges describe participant variation and are not confidence intervals. No significance tests or adjusted cohort effects are shown.

Blue denotes Petrus and coral denotes Kerr. This mapping adapts the blue/coral vector stroke colors observed in Figure 2 of the scWAT study (doi:10.1038/s41467-023-43021-8); the compact repeated categories and thin axes are also informed by Figure 2 of Vanneste et al., Nature Immunology (2023), doi:10.1038/s41590-023-01468-3. The shared-axis layout and outlined summary boxes are EasyViz design decisions. The source papers' biological groups and analysis methods are not transferred.

Source subtype labels are abbreviated for display: myC, myeloid cluster; LAM, lipid-associated macrophage; Mo, monocyte; DC2, dendritic cell type 2. Additional spacing and thin separators follow the published subtype annotations; they do not indicate estimated distances.

For the separate five-year transfer panel, only the 37 Kerr participants are included, and the change is five years minus baseline. The same row definitions, units, color scale and summaries apply; columns are re-ordered using the five-year changes.
