# Manuscript caption text

Growth inhibition profiles among *Carnobacterium maltaromaticum* strains. The heatmap shows 20 sender strains and 20 receiver strains selected independently at evenly spaced ranks of their mean growth inhibition index (GII) from the complete 76 × 76 source matrix. GII is the supplied growth-delay measure in minutes; negative values and self-pairs are retained. Marginal bars show arithmetic means across all 76 partners, rather than means of the displayed subset. Binary annotation strips identify strains marked as genome-analyzed in the supplied metadata. Selection spans the mean-response range and is not a random or prevalence-representative sample.

The diverging blue–white–gold–orange–coral scale adopts zero growth delay as its neutral reference and retains limits of −400 to 1,000 min without clipping. Its piecewise linear normalization maps −400 to 0, zero to 0.5, and 1,000 to 1: for raw GII x, the normalized coordinate is (x + 400)/800 below zero and 0.5 + x/2000 above zero. Colorbar ticks remain in original minutes; the inverse mapping is −400 + 800u for u ≤ 0.5 and 2000(u − 0.5) for u ≥ 0.5. Equal changes within either sign have equal color-coordinate changes, while the two signs have different slopes because their ranges differ. The separate mean-bar axes retain their numeric scales. The colors interpolate continuously through explicitly recorded color-coordinate stops: −400 min blue, −200 light blue, zero white, 100 pale gold, 250 gold, 500 orange, and 1,000 coral. These color stops encode sign and ordered magnitude; they do not bin measurements or introduce statistical thresholds or significance annotations. Both arms have monotonic sRGB relative luminance toward/away from the white center, respectively.

Data: Gontijo et al. (2022), DOI 10.3390/microorganisms10091794.
