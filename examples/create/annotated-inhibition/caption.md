# Manuscript caption text

Growth-delay profiles among *Carnobacterium maltaromaticum* strains. The heatmap shows 20 sender and 20 receiver strains selected independently at evenly spaced ranks of their mean growth inhibition index (GII) from the complete 76 × 76 source matrix. GII is the supplied difference in time to reach OD595 = 0.2 with cell-free supernatant added to fresh medium relative to fresh medium alone, in minutes. Positive values denote delay and negative values earlier attainment; the source paper uses GII > 300 min as its inhibition criterion. No new classification, significance test or growth-promotion inference is made. Self-pairs are retained. Selection spans the mean-response range and is not a random or prevalence-representative sample.

The single-hue blue scale is globally linear over −400 to 1,000 min, outward-rounded from the complete observed range −398.9071599 to 981.4179003 min. For GII x, the normalized color coordinate is (x + 400)/1400 and its inverse is −400 + 1400u. Zero is labeled on the colorbar, without a special color boundary or different slope. The continuous color map introduces no bins, clipping or statistical thresholds.

Marginal bars show arithmetic means over all 76 partners, rather than the displayed subset. Both use numeric limits 0–700 min. Their axes have different physical lengths and orientations, so comparisons should use tick values rather than uncalibrated physical bar length. Binary strips identify strains marked as genome-analyzed in the supplied metadata. Colors are EasyViz design choices, not sampled publication colors or transferred biological labels.

Data and measurement definition: Gontijo et al. (2022), DOI 10.3390/microorganisms10091794, Section 2.2. No author plotting code was used. Assign the final figure number and panel letter during manuscript assembly; this text remains separate from the panel.
