Absolute thermogenic-gene expression and descriptive genotype contrasts in
subcutaneous white adipose tissue. Each heatmap cell shows log2(TPM + 1), with
a pseudocount of 1 TPM, for one of six independent mice: YT-FF controls and
adipocyte-specific Yap/Taz-knockout YT-AKO mice (*n* = 3 per genotype). Genotype
bands identify the source samples F1–F3 and A1–A3. All 29 genes and 174 author-
supplied TPM values are retained in source-row order, without additional
upstream normalization or row standardization. The right lane shows the
YT-AKO mean minus the YT-FF mean of the three log-transformed values per gene;
these are descriptive log-expression differences, not DESeq2 fold changes or
significance tests. The six literal Atp5o zeros, reported as not detectable in
the original figure, remain observed zeros rather than missing values; its
zero difference is marked at the origin. Samples are unpaired. No adjusted
P values, significance symbols, or clustering are inferred. Data and the
design analogue are from Huang et al., “Transcriptional repression of beige
fat innervation via a YAP/TAZ-S100B axis”, Nature Communications 14, 7102 (2023),
[Figure 2b and Source Data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43021-8),
