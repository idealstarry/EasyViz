Participant BMI distributions in four selected source cohorts from Massier et al., Nature Communications (2023), Fig. 8a (DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-36983-2; source release https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/y3pxvr4xbf/2; CC BY 4.0). All 185 available values in Krieg (23), Petrus (29), Kerr (77) and Arner, E (56) are retained; none of these selected records has missing BMI. The other four source cohorts are outside this declared display. Boxes show the median and linearly interpolated 25th/75th percentiles; whiskers end at the most extreme observed value within 1.5 IQR. Every raw value, including values beyond the whiskers, remains drawn as a point. Categorical-only beeswarm placement preserves exact BMI coordinates on the 15–60 kg/m² axis. Cohort colors match their box outlines. No pairing, adjusted comparison or hypothesis test is inferred; field names are remapped for this transfer only.

Raw observations occupy an adjacent right-hand beeswarm lane rather than the box and whisker centers, keeping the sample distribution and summary endpoints separately readable. This categorical offset carries no numerical or additional-group meaning.
