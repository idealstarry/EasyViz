Total effects of mammographic-density phenotypes on breast-cancer outcomes. Circles show the 24 published odds ratios per SD increment in the indicated exposure; horizontal segments show the supplied 95% confidence intervals from univariable inverse-variance-weighted Mendelian randomization. The horizontal axis is logarithmic, and the dashed reference is an odds ratio of 1. Hollow circles retain the source table's interval-overlap state; filled circles retain its non-overlap state. The supplied estimates and asymmetric endpoints are unchanged. The source gives GWAS-level sample sizes, including 24,158 for mammographic density and 247,173 for overall breast cancer, and refers subtype sizes to a supplementary table; per-estimate sample sizes are not provided and remain unknown. ER, estrogen receptor; HER2, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2; SD, standard deviation; CI, confidence interval.
