Spike-specific IgG concentrations before and after vaccination in 127
participants, stratified by prior infection (64 participants, orange) or no
prior infection (63 participants, blue). Each point is one biological
measurement from an explicitly identified participant at one time point;
all 254 supplied values are retained. The prior-infection group includes
participants classified positive by history and/or nucleocapsid serology,
including four Source Data entries recorded as `yes/NCAP+`. After-vaccination
samples were scheduled 13–18 days after vaccination. Vertical positions use
a logarithmic display of BAU/ml (binding antibody units per millilitre).

Black horizontal lines indicate raw-scale medians; vertical segments and short
caps span Q1–Q3. Quartiles use the explicitly adopted Weibull estimator
(Hyndman–Fan type 6). These intervals describe the observations and are not
confidence intervals. The source does not name its quartile estimator; three
recomputed IQR widths round to the article's reported values, while the
no-prior-infection after-vaccination IQR is 4750.06 BAU/ml rather than its
reported rounded 4751. No significance test was performed for this derivative.
