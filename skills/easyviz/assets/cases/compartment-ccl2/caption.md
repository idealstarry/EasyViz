Ccl2 concentrations in lung and serum from IM-DTR mice and littermate controls
at 0, 12, 24 and 48 h after diphtheria toxin (DT) administration. Each open
circle (control) or open square (IM-DTR) represents one source mouse
observation; the short colored horizontal ticks and straight connecting
segments show group means, and the vertical intervals show mean ± SEM, calculated as sample SD
divided by the square root of the observed group size. Groups contain *n* = 4–8
observations, with all 88 source values retained. The source describes terminal
sampling and pooling from two independent experiments. Lines connect means
of different terminal time groups and do not track individual animals.

Lung concentration is expressed in pg/mg protein and serum concentration in
pg/ml. Their separately labeled axes retain different units and ranges;
absolute heights across the two panels are not directly comparable. Mean and
SEM anchors use the exact linear times 0, 12, 24 and 48 h. Raw observations
occupy small bounded display columns to the left (control) or right (IM-DTR)
of each nominal time; these horizontal offsets, at most 4.45 mm, prevent
symbol overlap and do not represent different sampling times. Concentration
coordinates are unchanged. The serum display extends slightly below zero
to retain the complete symbol for the smallest positive observation; no
negative concentration has been added. Mouse and experiment identifiers are
not supplied, so neither cross-time nor cross-compartment pairing is inferred.

The eight author-reported adjusted P values are preserved in
`inputs/author-adjusted-p.csv` with their workbook cell addresses. The source
caption attributes these results to two-way ANOVA with Tukey post hoc tests;
the scope of the correction family across compartments is not explicitly
specified. They are not independently recalculated or plotted as significance
stars in this descriptive Create case.
