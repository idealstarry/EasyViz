Serum IgG before and after vaccination for all 127 paired participants in Urschel et al., Nature Communications (2024), Fig. 2b (DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-47429-8; CC BY 4.0). Each point contains two measurements from the same person. Both axes use logarithmic display scales in BAU/ml; values are not transformed or imputed. Colors retain the source prior-infection grouping (63 without and 64 with prior infection). No correlation test, fitted trend, threshold, or independent-observation assumption is added.
