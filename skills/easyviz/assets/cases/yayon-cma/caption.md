Data-supported adaptation of Yayon, Kedlian, Boehme et al., Nature (2024), Fig. 3f. The original dendrogram is omitted because the published Source Data does not supply its topology or merge heights; this panel does not reproduce that layer or claim complete panel equivalence. Two heatmaps show the supplied normalized mean expression values for fetal and paediatric samples across ten thymic cortico-medullary-axis (CMA) regions, with the original order of 65 genes and a shared 0–1 scale. Bars show supplied cosine similarities between the two spatial expression patterns. Hollow circles on the zero line decode the supplied Bonferroni-corrected two-way ANOVA interaction P values for age group and CMA bin; circle area increases for P < 0.05, P < 0.01 and P < 0.001. The ANOVA, normalization, cosine similarities and clustering were not recalculated. Orange labels retain the source-caption emphasis on CCL21, CCL19, CXCL12 and CCL25; outlined ranges retain the published age-divergent gene emphasis. Label aliases change only the displayed spelling of Sub-Capsular and Medullar; literal source IDs remain unchanged. CMJ, cortico-medullary junction.
