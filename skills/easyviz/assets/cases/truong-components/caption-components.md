Adapted from Truong et al., *Nature Methods* 21, 455–464 (2024), Fig. 1b ([article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-023-02162-w), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). Colored segments show the arithmetic means of the supplied HDR, both-signal and mutEJ cell percentages. Points show the sum of these three components within each supplied biological replicate; intervals show the mean of those totals ± their sample SD (ddof = 1; n = 3 biological replicates per condition). These are total SD intervals, not uncertainty on individual stacked segments. All source values, including zero components, are retained without normalization. The two control totals are close to zero on this common percentage scale. Source Data spelling is used for condition labels. This adaptation separates the original dual axes and adds total replicate observations; original comparison stars are omitted. No inferential tests were recomputed.
