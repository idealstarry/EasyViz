# Adopted figure specification

This is an explicitly disclosed **create adaptation** of Fig. 1b, selected after an independent reading of the official crop. It does not claim pixel reproduction.

- Keep all 7 conditions, all 3 supplied biological replicates per condition, all 63 component values and all 21 supplied ratio values. Do not normalize components or recalculate ratios.
- Use two independently exported 150 × 100 mm panels, with Arial 8 pt for axes, ticks and guide text. No in-image title, panel letter, prose note, or significance annotation.
- Stack the mean HDR, both-signal and mutEJ component percentages in the source's visible bottom-to-top order. Add all 21 per-replicate **total percentages**, each calculated by summing that replicate's three components. The one interval at the stack top is **mean total ± sample SD of the per-replicate totals** (ddof = 1). It is not a shifted component SD or a CI.
- Export ratios separately using the 21 supplied values, mean ± sample SD, all 21 raw points, and the same horizontal condition order. Retain the two source-declared low-event controls with hatched bars and a small state guide; their values remain visible.
- Raw points use filled borderless circles, fixed geometric area 7 pt² in the stacked/ratio panels and 4 pt² in the grouped alternative, with stable unit-ID offsets. The grouped view has three components per condition and uses the explicitly smaller marks at its wider canvas. Points are not connected; within-condition replicate ordinals do not establish cross-condition pairing.
- Use source-related muted green/peach/rose fills with a modest increase in contrast. Guides sit above the data area. Thin dark SD strokes and points remain readable above the fills; omit the grid.
- Use prepared Source Data spelling for condition labels. The published raster's KU label differs from the source workbook, so the source wording is recorded explicitly.
- The grouped alternative exports at 175 × 100 mm and shows each component's mean, its own raw values and its own sample SD. It is a further create adaptation, not a third published panel.

Differences from the paper are two single-scale outputs instead of dual axes, total-value points and total SD on the stacked panel, separate state guide, source spelling, increased fill contrast, simplified condition labels, and no unverified comparison stars. Narrative and statistical caveats are in the separate captions.
