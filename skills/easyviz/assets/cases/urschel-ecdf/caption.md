Empirical cumulative distributions of the supplied spike-specific IgG measurements before and after vaccination. Each step represents the fraction of measurements at or below its horizontal position. Each time-point distribution has 127 distinct values, so each observed jump is 1/127; this dataset has no tied measurements within either distribution. The horizontal axis is logarithmic and the measurements retain their original BAU/ml units. All 127 participants and both measurements per participant are included (254 observations): 64 with prior infection and 63 without prior infection. Each observation is one participant's measurement at one time point. The two source-positive infection classifications are pooled according to the paper's definition. This view pools infection groups to compare the overall marginal distributions; it does not display individual pairing, stratified effects or an inferential comparison. No smoothing, fitted distribution, hypothesis test, filtering, imputation or pseudocount was used.

This is a new EasyViz view of Source Data from Urschel et al., Nature Communications 15, 3077 (2024), DOI [10.1038/s41467-024-47429-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47429-8), distributed under CC BY 4.0. It is not a reconstruction of a published ECDF. Colors encode sampling time in this design, whereas the original Figure 2b uses color for prior-infection status.
