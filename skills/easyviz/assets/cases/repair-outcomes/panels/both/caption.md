This individual panel compares HDR and mutEJ within treatments. Neutral open bars are arithmetic means, intervals sample SD (ddof = 1), and dots all biological replicates ($n = 3$ per treatment). Original percentages are retained. DMSO is the vehicle; NU = NU-7441 (1 µM), KU = KU-0060548 (0.25 µM), L755 = L755507 (5 µM), and SCR7 = SCR7 pyrazine (1 µM). Source: Truong et al., Nature Methods, Fig. 1b Source Data (DOI 10.1038/s41592-023-02162-w; CC BY 4.0). No cross-condition pairing, normalization or hypothesis test is introduced.
