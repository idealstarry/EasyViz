Supplied differential-expression results comparing source cell clusters C3 and C5, adapted from Xiang et al., Nature Communications 15, 2164 (2024), Figure 3E, DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-46480-9. Each row retains its supplied median difference (C3 minus C5) and −log10 adjusted P value. Circle fill area is proportional to the supplied expression probability on a common 0–1 scale; 37 supplied zero-probability rows have zero circle area and remain in the accompanying data. Blue and red denote lower and higher expression in C3, respectively. Grey denotes an absolute median difference below 0.2; all 1,457 supplied rows already have adjusted P below 0.01, so grey does not denote P-nonsignificance. Dashed lines indicate adopted median-difference boundaries ±0.2 and adjusted-P boundary 0.01. These values and source classes were not recalculated; this plotting-only adaptation does not reconstruct the upstream filtering universe, independent units or differential test. Adjacent pathway tables and the source panel letter are omitted. Source data and reference excerpt are attributed under CC BY 4.0; labels, dimensions, palette estimates and geometry are adaptations. Two source gene identifiers are stored as Excel dates; their decoded values are retained without guessing the original symbols.
